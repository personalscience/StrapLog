StrapLog

Beat-by-beat heart-rate variability from your chest strap, for iPhone and Apple Watch.

StrapLog records every heartbeat from a chest-strap heart-rate sensor and keeps it safe on your iPhone. It shows heart rate, RMSSD and DFA α1 live, saves each recording as a file you own, and lets you mark moments as they happen.

StrapLog requires a Polar H10-compatible heart-rate sensor. Watches and wrist sensors do not connect.

The full manual is in the app: tap ? at the top right of Home.

Common questions

The heart stays grey. StrapLog is still searching. Wet the strap's electrodes and put it on (the sensor only wakes on skin), check that Bluetooth is on and that StrapLog is allowed to use it in Settings → StrapLog. Close other apps that may be connected to the strap.

I have a new strap. StrapLog connects only to the first strap it found. Tap the heart, then Use a Different Strap.

Where are my recordings? Each recording is one SQLite file in Files → iCloud Drive → StrapLog, or Files → On My iPhone → StrapLog if iCloud Drive is off. Any SQLite tool opens them.

Will I lose recordings if I reinstall? Files in iCloud Drive come back automatically when StrapLog opens. Files in On My iPhone are deleted with the app, so copy them first.

There are gaps in my overnight recording. Keep the phone within a few metres of the bed, wear the strap snug with wet electrodes, and replace the strap battery if it is low.

StrapLog is a personal measurement tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
